Monthly Priorities

1 · Sleep

The single most important thing right now. Without sleep, there is no progress.

2 · Gymnastics

Quality > volume. Three clean reps beat ten sloppy ones.

3 · Snatch

Don't grind it out. Stay sharp and technical.

4 · Running

The goal: run well on tired legs.

5 · Recovery

Mobility · Electrolytes · Stretching · Breathwork · Sauna if available.

Bottom Line

You have a strong base. Show up fresh, sharp, and efficient with gymnastics under fatigue — you'll be dangerous in the heats.
